North Central Province

The Cultural Triangle — the island's ancient royal heartland.

Explore the places ↓ All 30 destinations

Two great capitals, two thousand years: Anuradhapura's sacred Bodhi tree and giant dagobas, Polonnaruwa's Gal Vihara Buddhas, and between them Sigiriya's lion rock and Dambulla's painted caves.
