Wellness & Ayurveda

Slow down: Ayurveda, yoga by the sea and cool hill-country air.

See the places ↓ Plan this trip

Sri Lanka has practised Ayurveda for thousands of years, and its yoga culture has bloomed along the south coast. Combine beach mornings with herbal treatments, or disappear into the cool tea hills for a slower kind of rest. This is the island at its most healing.

Signature experiences

🧘

Beach yoga in Hiriketiya

Sunrise classes over the horseshoe cove — studios for every level, plus breathwork and sound baths.

🌿

Ayurveda treatments

Herbal massages, steam and guided detox in certified centres — book multi-day programmes in advance.

🛶

Bolgoda calm

Lakeside paddling, birdsong and silence minutes from Colombo.

🌫

Nuwara Eliya air

Cool pines, tea gardens and colonial-era rest in the island's high station.

☕

Haputale mornings

Mist, tea and long breakfasts above the clouds — the slowest town in the hills.
