Wildlife & Safaris

Leopards in Yala, elephants in Minneriya, blue whales off Mirissa — the island is one giant safari.

See the places ↓ Plan this trip

Few islands pack this much wildlife into one loop: the world's densest leopard population in Yala, wild elephant gatherings in Minneriya, blue whales minutes from the south coast, and coral reefs in the east. Safari at dawn, bring binoculars, and keep at least two full days for wildlife.

Signature experiences

🐆

Yala leopard safari

Block 1 is famous for leopards, with elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles and a wild coastline in the same drive.

🐘

Minneriya elephant gathering

Hundreds of wild elephants around the reservoir from July to October — one of Asia's great wildlife spectacles.

🐋

Blue whales of Mirissa

November to April, these waters are among the world's most reliable for blue whales — with dolphins as a bonus.

🐠

Pigeon Island, Trincomalee

Snorkel coral gardens and glide with reef sharks and turtles in the calm eastern sea.

🦩

Bolgoda wetlands

Mangroves, kingfishers and monitor lizards just minutes from Colombo — a quiet half-day escape.
