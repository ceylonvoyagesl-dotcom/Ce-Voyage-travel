Adventure & Outdoors

Hike cloud-forest peaks, raft wild rivers, surf Indian Ocean breaks — Sri Lanka plays hard.

See the places ↓ Plan this trip

Mist-topped peaks, jungle rivers and a surf coast that wraps the island — Sri Lanka is a playground. Hike at sunrise, raft after rain, and ride the famous Kandy–Ella train with the doors open (safely). Every adventure here pairs with a cold king coconut at the end.

Signature experiences

🥾

Ella hikes

Little Adam's Peak, Ella Rock and the Nine Arch Bridge walk — cloud-forest trails for every level.

🚣

Kitulgala white-water rafting

Rapids on the Kelani river — the river where The Bridge on the River Kwai was filmed.

🏄

Surf Arugam Bay

Beginner lessons on soft waves to world-class point breaks, April to October.

🌄

Adam's Peak

The night climb from Maskeliya to sunrise on the sacred summit — 5,500 steps, unforgettable.

💦

Waterfall country

Ramboda and Haputale falls, cool dips and the tea-country train through the clouds.
