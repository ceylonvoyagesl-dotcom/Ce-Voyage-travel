Food & Local Life

Rice and curry, hoppers at dawn, crab in Jaffna — eat your way around the island.

See the places ↓ Plan this trip

Sri Lankan cooking is the island's real map: coconut-rich rice and curry in the south, fiery crab in the north, hoppers at dawn everywhere. Eat where the locals queue, take a cooking class, and you will understand the country one mouthful at a time.

Signature experiences

🍛

Colombo street food

Kottu roti, egg hoppers and market chaos — Pettah by day, Galle Face at sunset.

🌶

Kandy spice country

Spice gardens, tea tastings and the Kandyan dance — culture on a plate.

🥘

Galle cooking class

Market tour, mortar and pestle, then a home-cooked feast inside the fort.

🦀

Jaffna crab curry

The north's famous fiery specialities — crab, odiyal kool and palmyrah sweets.

🌾

Mahiyanganaya village life

Farm visits, village lunches and the island's indigenous heritage.
