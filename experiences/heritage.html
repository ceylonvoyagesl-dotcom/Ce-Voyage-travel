Ancient Heritage

Two thousand years of kingdoms, temples and sacred trees — the story of Sri Lanka in stone.

See the places ↓ Plan this trip

Sri Lanka's Cultural Triangle holds one of Asia's greatest concentrations of ancient sites — royal rock fortresses, colossal dagobas, cave temples and living places of worship that have been in use for over two thousand years. Go early in the morning, dress for temples, and give each site the time it deserves.

Signature experiences

🦁

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

A 5th-century palace on a granite monolith. Climb for the frescoes, the Mirror Wall and jungle views to the horizon.

🛕

Temple of the Tooth, Kandy

The island's most sacred shrine on the shore of Kandy Lake — come for the evening puja when drums and fire fill the hall.

🕉

Anuradhapura Sacred City

Sri Maha Bodhi, giant dagobas and vast monastic ruins of the island's first capital. Explore by bicycle.

🗿

Polonnaruwa

The medieval capital: the Gal Vihara rock Buddhas, royal bathing pools and ancient council chambers.

⛰

Dambulla Cave Temples

Five caves carved into the rock, holding over 150 Buddha statues and centuries of painted ceilings.
