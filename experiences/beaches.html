Beaches & Islands

Golden sand, surf towns and calm eastern bays — choose your coast by the season.

See the places ↓ Plan this trip

The south and west shine from December to April; the east coast takes over from May to September — so there is always a perfect beach somewhere. From lazy golden bays to surf meccas and calm snorkelling islands, Sri Lanka's 1,340 km of coast has a mood for every traveller.

Signature experiences

🏖

Mirissa

Golden bay, beach cafés and whale-watching boats — the classic southern beach town.

🏄

Hiriketiya

A horseshoe surf cove with yoga studios, espresso bars and easy-going surf.

🌊

Arugam Bay

Asia's great point breaks, April to October — plus lagoons and endless sand.

🐢

Trincomalee

Calm turquoise water, Pigeon Island snorkelling and natural hot springs.

⛵

Matara & Dondra Head

The southernmost point of the island, a walled Dutch town and quiet local beaches.
