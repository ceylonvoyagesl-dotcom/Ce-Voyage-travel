The 10-Day Classic Tour

Ancient cities, tea-country trains, wild safaris and southern beaches — the best of Sri Lanka in one loop.

Book this tour 🖨 Print / Save as PDF

Day-by-day itinerary

A balanced route of ~1,200 km with short drives, two rail journeys and stays in our favourite hand-picked hotels. Fully customisable — swap days, add nights, or travel in reverse.

1
Arrival · Negombo

Welcome to Sri Lanka

Land at Colombo Bandaranaike Airport, where your driver welcomes you with a sign. Short transfer to a beach hotel in Negombo to rest, swim and enjoy your first Sri Lankan dinner.

2
Sigiriya

Lion Rock & the Cultural Triangle

Drive into the green heart of the island. Late-afternoon climb of the Sigiriya rock fortress for sunset over the jungle — the frescoes, the Mirror Wall and the Lion's Paws.

3
Dambulla · Minneriya · Polonnaruwa

Cave Temples & Wild Elephants

Morning at the Dambulla cave temples, then an afternoon safari in Minneriya or Kaudulla National Park, famous for its wild elephant gatherings. Overnight near Sigiriya.

4
Kandy

The Sacred City

Drive to Kandy, the last royal capital. Visit the Temple of the Tooth, stroll around Kandy Lake, and end the day with a traditional Kandyan dance performance.

5
Nuwara Eliya

Into the Tea Country

Board the morning train for one of the world's great rail journeys, winding through tea plantations and cloud forest. Tea factory visit and tasting in Nuwara Eliya, the old colonial hill station.

6
Ella

Hills, Trains & Waterfalls

Continue by rail or road to Ella. Walk the Nine Arch Bridge, hike Little Adam's Peak for valley views, and cool off at Ravana Falls. Free evening in Ella's cafés.

7
Yala

Leopards & Wildlife Safari

Descend to the south-east for an afternoon jeep safari in Yala National Park — leopards, elephants, sloth bears and a wild coastline. Overnight in a safari lodge.

8
Mirissa · Galle

The Southern Beaches

Drive along the south coast to Mirissa. Optional early-morning whale watching (November–April), then beach time. Sunset walk on the ramparts of the Galle Fort.

9
Colombo

Capital Day

Return to Colombo by the coast road or the ocean-side train. A city tour of the markets, the Lotus Tower and Galle Face Green, with time for souvenir shopping.

10
Departure

Ayubowan — until next time

Breakfast at the hotel, then transfer to the airport for your flight home — with a phone full of memories and a promise to return.
