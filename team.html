Guides & Team

Professional guides, careful drivers and hiking supporters — the friendly faces of your trip.

Meet the roles ↓ Request a guide

Every person in our network is licensed, background-checked and chosen for how they treat guests. This page shows the roles we staff — photos, names and live reviews are published per guide on request.

Our roles

🏛

National Guide

The top licence in Sri Lankan tourism (SLTDA). Deep history and culture — perfect for the Cultural Triangle and multi-day tours in French or English.

🚗

Chauffeur Guide

Driver and guide in one: the island's favourite format. Drives safely, narrates the journey and knows every tea shop along the way.

📍

Local Guide

Born and raised in the region — the insider who opens doors in Jaffna, Ella, Galle or wherever you are.

🥾

Hiking Supporter

Trained trekking partners for Adam's Peak, the Knuckles and Horton Plains — first-aid certified, mountain-wise.

🛞

Driver

Punctual, careful, police-verified drivers for transfers and full tours — the quiet heroes of every itinerary.
